# Sample teaching handout — DEMO ONLY
This is a short, synthetic example written for testing EasyStudyAI. It is NOT a professor's official lecture.

Learning goal: connect stroke volume, heart rate and cardiac output.

Cardiac output is the volume of blood pumped by one ventricle per minute.
Cardiac output = heart rate × stroke volume (CO = HR × SV).
Heart rate is the number of beats per minute.
Stroke volume is the volume ejected by one ventricle per beat.
